How it works

Four moves, zero babysitting

Install it once. It runs in your tray and does the rest on its own.

01 · DETECT

Knows you're gaming

Recognizes your games automatically — Steam, Epic, GOG, Riot, Xbox — no setup, no lists to maintain.

02 · HOLD

Holds the noise

Gmail, Slack, and Discord get caught and queued instead of buzzing you mid-match.

03 · BREAK THROUGH

Emergencies still land

Truly urgent messages pop a small overlay over your game — visible, audible, never focus-stealing.

04 · RECAP

One clean catch-up

Quit the game and get a single recap: how long you played, what came in, what needs action.

Features

Everything, and nothing in your way

Auto game detection

Reads your launchers' own libraries for the real game name and artwork. Any game, automatically.

Smart priority routing

VIP senders and urgent keywords break through; everything else waits. Deterministic rules, optional AI on top.

Overlay that respects the game

Top-corner, tuned duration, sound optional, and immune to the fullscreen silencing that mutes normal notifications.

One-click connectors

Connect Gmail, Discord, and more from a real dashboard — connect and disconnect with a click.

Yours to tune

Break-through rules, quiet do-not-disturb, sound, overlay timing, dark or light — all in Settings.

Updates itself

A quiet progress bar, then it restarts on the new version. No reinstalls, ever.
