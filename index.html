How it works

Four moves, zero babysitting

Install it once. It runs in your tray and does the rest on its own.

01 · DETECT

Knows you're gaming

Recognizes most games automatically — Steam, Epic, GOG, Riot, Xbox. The occasional oddly-named launcher can be added in one line of config.

02 · HOLD

Holds the noise

Gmail and Discord get caught and queued instead of buzzing you mid-match.

03 · BREAK THROUGH

Emergencies still land

Truly urgent messages pop a small overlay over your game — visible, audible, never focus-stealing.

04 · RECAP

One clean catch-up

Quit the game and get a single recap: how long you played, what came in, what needs action.

Features

Everything, and nothing in your way

Auto game detection

Reads your launchers' own libraries (Steam, Epic, GOG) for the real game name — with box art for Steam titles and a best-effort lookup for the rest.

Smart priority routing

VIP senders and urgent keywords break through; everything else waits. Deterministic rules, optional AI on top.

Overlay that respects the game

Top-corner, tuned duration, sound optional, and drawn topmost so it isn't muted by Focus Assist the way normal notifications are. (A game in exclusive fullscreen can still cover it — borderless/windowed shows it every time.)

Dashboard-managed connectors

Once a connector's credentials are set up, turn Gmail, Discord, and more on or off from a real dashboard with a click — no config-file editing or service restarts.

Yours to tune

Break-through rules, quiet do-not-disturb, sound, overlay timing, dark or light — all in Settings.

Open & auditable

Every line is on GitHub — read it, fork it, or run the built-in updater on a source install.
