What GameGate does

GameGate watches your Windows notifications - Discord, Slack, email, and anything else that pops up - so it can quietly hold them while you're gaming and hand you one clean recap when you're done.

Your privacy

GameGate runs entirely on THIS computer. It reads your notifications locally, on your PC, to build your recap. Nothing about your notifications is uploaded or leaves your machine.

By clicking Next you agree to let GameGate read your Windows notifications for this purpose. You can pause it from the tray icon, or uninstall it anytime.
